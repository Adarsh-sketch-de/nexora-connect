How to use Connect

1. Create an account

Sign up with a username, email and password, then complete your profile.

2. Find another user

Open Discover, search for their username, and send a connection request.

3. Accept the request

The other user opens Requests and accepts your invitation.

4. Start chatting or meeting

Use Messages for persistent chat or Meeting Room for browser video.

5. Generate a summary

In a conversation, click Generate summary to create saved key points and suggestions.
