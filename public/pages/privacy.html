Privacy Policy

Nexora stores account information, profile data, connection relationships, messages, images, summaries and notification records needed to provide the service.

Passwords are stored as cryptographic hashes, not plain text. Uploaded images are stored on the development server in this build.

Admin access to conversation content is intended only for controlled moderation/support and is recorded in audit logs.

For production, add retention rules, data export/deletion workflows, encrypted object storage, backups and a jurisdiction-appropriate privacy policy.
